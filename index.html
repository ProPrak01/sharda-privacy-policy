Privacy Policy for Sharda Academy App

At Sharda Academy, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect the information you provide while using our mobile application.

1. Information we collect

When you use the Sharda Academy app, we may collect the following information:

  • Mobile Number: Required for login via OTP.
  • Email Address: Optional; provided at user's choice.
  • Date of Birth and Gender: Used for personalization and reporting.
  • Educational Background: Such as 12th grade, graduation, etc., used to tailor content and courses.

We do not collect or track your location (GPS) or use your microphone, camera or photos.

2. How we use your information

The information collected is used to:

  • Verify your identity and provide secure access to the app.
  • Personalize your experience by recommending suitable courses and test series.
  • Track your course progress and performance.
  • Improve app functionality and user experience through analytics.

3. Analytics

We use third-party services such as Amplitude to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how users interact with the app so we can improve its performance and features.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. Data security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

However, please understand that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your choices

  • You can choose to skip entering your email during registration.
  • You can update or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop all data collection.

6. Children's privacy

Our app is intended for users above the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware of such data being collected, we will take steps to delete it.
