Privacy Policy

Sharda Academy

Important: This privacy policy explains how Sharda Academy collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our educational services.

1. Information We Collect

Sharda Academy is committed to collecting only the minimal data necessary to provide you with the best educational experience. We collect the following information:

  • Mobile Number: Required for account verification and important notifications
  • Email Address: Optional, used for account recovery and communication
  • Date of Birth (DOB): To ensure age-appropriate content and compliance with regulations
  • Gender: For personalized content recommendations
  • Education Level: To customize learning materials and track academic progress

What We Don't Collect

We respect your privacy and do NOT collect or track location data or GPS information:

  • Location data or GPS tracking
  • Browsing history from other websites
  • Social media activity
  • Financial information beyond what's necessary for transactions

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Verify Users: To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Personalize Content: To recommend relevant courses and learning materials
  • Improve Services: To enhance our educational platform and user experience
  • Communication: To send important updates about your courses and account
  • Support: To provide customer service and technical assistance

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We are committed to protecting your privacy:

  • No Advertising Partnerships: We do not share your data with advertisers
  • No Data Sales: We never sell your personal information to third parties
  • Limited Sharing: Data may only be shared with trusted service providers who help us operate our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect our users' safety

4. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your personal data:

  • Access: View all personal data we have about you
  • Update: Modify your information at any time through your account settings
  • Delete: Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download a copy of your personal data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from non-essential communications

Easy Data Management: You can manage or delete your data anytime through your account settings or by contacting our support team.

5. Age Restrictions

Sharda Academy is designed for users aged 13 years and older. If you are under 13 years old, you may not use our services. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

For users between 13-17 years old, we recommend parental supervision and involvement in the learning process.

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Secure data centers with physical security measures

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. When you delete your account, we will permanently remove your data within 30 days, except for information we are legally required to retain.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic platform functionality
  • Performance Cookies: Help us improve user experience (you can opt-out)
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not use cookies for advertising purposes

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes through:

  • Email notification (if you've provided an email address)
  • In-app notifications
  • Updates on our website
